# Profile and settings

**Settings** is at the bottom of the sidebar, next to your email.

## Appearance

**Light**, **Dark** or **System**. System, the default, follows your
device's setting and switches when it does. Your choice is saved in this
browser and applied before the page draws, so there's no flash on reload.

## Instrument metal

Changes the colour of the sidebar's small section labels: Brass (the
default), Copper, Iron, Silver or Indigo. Nothing else changes. Saved in
this browser.

## Change password

Enter your current password and a new one of at least 8 characters.

Changing your password doesn't sign out your other sessions. An owner can
end them under **Admin → Active sessions**. See
[Sign-in and sessions](sign-in.md).

## Other per-browser settings

These are remembered in the browser too, not on your account:

- **Technical / Simple** mode (sidebar toggle). See
  [Simple mode](../start/simple-mode.md).
- The workspace picked in the **station switcher**.
- Whether the sidebar is **collapsed**.

Signing in from another browser starts with the defaults.

## Your email and role

Your email and role (owner or member) are set by an owner. There's no
self-service way to change your email; ask an owner to create a new account
for you. See [Workspaces and roles](../teams/workspaces-and-roles.md).
